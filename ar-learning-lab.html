AR Learning Laboratory

A web-based Augmented Reality app that lets students explore computer equipment — monitor, keyboard, mouse and printer — by placing virtual devices over the live camera view.

How to use

  • Tap Start Camera and allow access (phone or PC webcam).
  • Tap a tab (Monitor, Keyboard, Mouse, Printer) to place that device.
  • Drag to move. Pinch with two fingers to resize and rotate.
  • Use the side buttons to rotate, enlarge, shrink or delete the selected device.
  • Tap the numbered chips in the info card to learn what each part does.
  • PC: wheel = resize, Shift + wheel = rotate, Del = remove, arrows = nudge.
  • 📸 saves a picture of your AR scene.

Technologies

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, WebRTC camera access (getUserMedia), SVG graphics, Canvas, Pointer Events (touch + mouse), localStorage.

Learning objectives

  • Identify common input and output devices.
  • Name the main parts of each device and what they do.
